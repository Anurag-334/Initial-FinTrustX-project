Deployed Model: XGBoost

FinTrustX deploys a tuned XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) classifier trained with scale_pos_weight on highly imbalanced credit default data.

  • Task: Binary Credit Default Classification (TARGET = 1 for Default)
  • Preprocessing: ColumnTransformer handling 121 raw inputs $\to$ 245 preprocessed features
  • Benchmark ROC-AUC: 0.7610 on held-out test data
  • Explainability Engine: Tree SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for transparent adverse action codes

About FinTrustX & Governance

FinTrustX is an explainable AI platform designed to assist credit risk underwriters. The system executes the following workflow:

  • 1. Collects applicant demographic, financial, and credit bureau inputs.
  • 2. Applies leakage-safe imputation and feature transformations.
  • 3. Runs XGBoost to estimate default risk probability.
  • 4. Computes localized SHAP attributions and presents reason codes.

Decision-support disclaimer: FinTrustX is an automated risk intelligence tool intended to assist credit decisioning and should not be used as the sole basis for real-world lending without human underwriter review and regulatory compliance controls.
